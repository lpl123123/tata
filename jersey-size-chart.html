Jersey Size Charts

Finding the right fit is key to comfort and style. Use our charts below to determine the best size for you. If you're between sizes or prefer a looser fit, we recommend ordering one size up.

How to Measure

  • Chest/Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, under the armpits. Keep the tape measure horizontal.
  • Waist: Measure around your natural waistline, typically the narrowest part of your torso.
  • Hips: Stand with your feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips.
  • Length (Kids): Measure from the highest point of the shoulder down to the bottom hem of the jersey.
  • Suitable for Height (Kids): Choose the size that best matches your child's height for the most comfortable fit.
